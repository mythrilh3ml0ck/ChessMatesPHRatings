Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how your personal information is handled in the Chessmates Philippine Ratings application.

1. Information Collection and Use

We believe in privacy. Our application is designed to be a lightweight viewing tool for Chessmates Philippine Ratings and Philippine National Ratings.

The app DOES NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal data, including but not limited to:

  • Personal names or contact details
  • Location data (GPS)
  • Photos or Media files
  • Device identifiers
  • Microphone or Camera access

2. Age Appropriateness

This application is rated for Ages 3 and above. The content is educational and informational in nature, specifically focused on chess rating statistics. There is no social interaction, user-generated content, or advertising within the app.

3. Data Usage

The application only fetches publicly available chess rating CSV files from our secure repository to provide you with the latest rating updates. No user data is ever sent back to our servers.

4. External Links

The app may contain links to external websites (such as tournament results or official profiles). Once you leave our app, we are not responsible for the privacy practices of those external sites.

5. Contact

If you have any questions regarding this policy, you may contact the developer through official channels.
