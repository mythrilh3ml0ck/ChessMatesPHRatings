Our Mission

Chessmates Philippines is dedicated to empowering the local chess community through data-driven insights and professional rating systems. We strive to provide a fair and transparent platform for players to track their progress and compete at the highest level.

Official Rating System

Team Ranking

Ranking based on accumulated points from the Grand Prix point system.
